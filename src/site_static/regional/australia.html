  G'day from Melbourne.

  In 1986, Triple J aired an unlisted late-night satellite
  feed from Orion. Several of us taped it on TDK SA90s.
  Running the signal through stardust.c gives:

    STARDUST/1 KESTREL NODE 12 OF 14 -- HOLD FOR RELAY
    SOUTHERN SKIES HEAR THE ECHO FIRST

  The vinyl pressing here was pressed by Festival Records
  (Cat: OR-OZ85). The runout groove etching has the same
  carrier pitch. We're keeping the antennas pointed north.

  -- Lachlan & Bec, APANA St Kilda Node
