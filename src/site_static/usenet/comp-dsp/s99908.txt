how can a 14.2001 Hz sub-carrier survive modern digital transmission?"

introduced during analog-to-digital mastering.

where f_c = 14.2001 Hz and phi(t) in {0, pi} is the DBPSK symbol stream.

When this composite signal passes through analog vacuum tube compressors, magnetic
tape saturation, or vinyl cutter heads, the transfer function is non-linear:
   y(t) = alpha_1 * x(t) + alpha_2 * x(t)^2 + alpha_3 * x(t)^3 + ...

The quadratic term x(t)^2 generates cross-products:
   2 * s_audio(t) * A * cos(2 * pi * f_c * t + phi(t))

Notice that this convolves the entire audible spectrum of Nova Hart's vocals with
the 14.2 Hz DBPSK sub-carrier! The phase information phi(t) is thus mirrored

and 1500 Hz +/- 14.2 Hz).
