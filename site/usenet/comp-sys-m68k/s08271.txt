At ROM offset 0x0909, the string 'M.H. // THE HATCH IS OPEN // ADVANCED ACOUSTICS LAB' is stored in ASCII.

Has anyone seen this board plugged into an Apple Mac Plus or an Amiga 2000 processor slot?
