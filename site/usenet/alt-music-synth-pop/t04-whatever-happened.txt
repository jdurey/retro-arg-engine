I interviewed Nova Hart once, in October 1984, over an Orion satellite
link: I sat in their LA press office with a headset and she answered
live on the monitor. Her handlers sat behind me the whole time.

She wouldn't talk about the record. She only wanted to talk about
shortwave -- numbers stations -- and something she called 'the crack'.
She said 'some voices are too heavy for the world they're in'. When I
asked where she grew up she said 'West Berlin' a half-second too late.

Her last Live-Fidelity Broadcast was 30 September 1989.

M.H. is Marcus Hale, 'technical engineer' on the sleeve. I tried to find
him in 1990. Orion's lawyers found me first.
