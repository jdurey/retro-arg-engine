Strange one for the group. I've been running FFTs on digitized LPs for a
term project (TMS32010 eval board, decimating to 64 Hz for the LF work).

Nova Hart's SYNTHESIS (Orion ORX-0909, 1985) shows a stable spectral line
at 14.2 Hz, about 52 dB below program level. It is on every track of both
discs. It is NOT on the other 40-odd LPs I've tested, including her own
1984 'Nova-chan' record and the later hard rock ones.

Before anyone says rumble: it's also on the 1987 CD. No platter.

Phase hops between two states roughly every 0.56 s. That smells like BPSK
to me, but I'm a 2nd-year grad student, so tell me why I'm wrong.

servos that hunt. A 14 Hz servo artifact surviving a digital transfer is
entirely plausible -- the CD was mastered from the analog tape, Tom.

semester of TA hours this is mechanical.
