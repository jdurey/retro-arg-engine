Since Orion's 'resting' press release on 15 December the carrier traffic
has outgrown this group, and a lot of it isn't DSP any more. People are
worried about her. I don't think that is off-topic for human beings,
but it is off-topic for comp.dsp.

So: there is now a LISTSERV list at Ashford Poly.

    To join, send mail to LISTSERV@ASHPOLY (BITNET)
    or listserv@ashpoly.edu (Internet), with the single line:

        SUBSCRIBE NOVA-L Your Full Name

Monthly logs will be kept: 'GET NOVA-L LOG9001' and so on.

38 subscribers as of this morning, from 6 countries. Decode results,
pressing reports and stardust.c ports should still come here.
