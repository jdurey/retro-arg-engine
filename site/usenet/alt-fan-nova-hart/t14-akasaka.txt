  =====================================================================
    ORION RECORDS 'AKASAKA BRANCH' -- RECOVERED TAPES
    オリオン・レコード赤坂支社  回収テープ
    ftp.kamome-net.or.jp:/pub/nova/akasaka/                1995-05-22
  =====================================================================

  Recovered audio files from the Orion Records 'Akasaka Branch' (Tokyo).

  In 1985, to comply with the 'Public Harmony Acts', Orion Records
  sanitized Nova Hart's industrial sound for the Japanese market. They
  replaced the distortion with synthesizers and the rage with a smile.
  These 'Idol Protocol' recordings were designed to mask the glitches
  in her code.

    /!\ WARNING: Despite the upbeat production, emotional anomalies remain.

  Contains:
    audio/    City Pop / Kayokyoku remixes & static outtakes (.mp3 / Sun .au)
    visual/   Recovered 1980s anime visuals (frame grabs, U-matic promo)
    docs/     Declassified 'Orion Japan' assets

  HOW THESE WERE FOUND
  The Akasaka Branch closed in March 1993. Its tape library was sold by
  weight to a junk dealer under the tracks in Akihabara. On 20 May 1995 I
  bought one box (12 x 1/4-inch reels, 2 x U-matic KCA cassettes) for
  3,000 yen. Reel labels: 'IDOL PROTOCOL / NOVA-CHAN / 85'.

  Excerpts only. The full transfers stay with KAMOME-NET #14 while Orion
  is still sending letters. The end of U-matic cassette 1 is not posted.
  Read docs/reel_log.txt and you will understand why.

  The 14.2 Hz carrier is present on every reel. Somebody refused to take
  it out. Read docs/AK-85-031.txt, especially the margin.

  日本語：
  1985年、オリオン赤坂支社は「公共調和法」に従って、
  ノヴァ・ハートのインダストリアルな音を日本市場向けに作り変えました。
  ディストーションはシンセサイザーに、怒りは笑顔に。
  でも、感情の異常は残っています。
