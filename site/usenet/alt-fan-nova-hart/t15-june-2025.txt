Transfer log for the two U-matic cassettes. Please read to the end.

  U-MATIC KCA-30 x 2 -- 'NOVA-CHAN PROMO / TV-90 / IDOL PROTOCOL'
  Transfer log, Y. Tanabe, 1995-05-27. Sony VO-5850 -> PC-9801 frame grabber

  CASSETTE 1 (version A, 90 s)
  TC        CONTENT
  00:00:00  Colour bars, 1 kHz tone. Slate: 'ORION JAPAN / NOVA-CHAN / TV-90 / 85.04'
  00:00:10  Animated opening. Pastel night city, neon, starfield.
            Grab: visual/novachan_tv90_f0420.gif
  00:00:42  Nova-chan close-up, smiling. Frame 1263 (one field only): her
            eyes glow electric blue -- light coming out of them, not
            reflected. Grab: visual/novachan_tv90_f1263.gif
  00:01:30  ORION JAPAN logo. End of programme.
  00:01:31  Black. One second of heavy analogue static. Not dropout --
            the RF envelope is clean on either side.
  00:01:32  A whisper, female, very close to the microphone. In English,
            on a Japanese reel. Transcription agreed by four listeners:

              "They're formatting the grid. I'm losing the heavy.
               But keep the channels open. I will see you in June 2025."

  00:01:38  Black. Silence. Tape runs blank to the end.

  CASSETTE 2 (version B, 60 s cut-down): no static, no whisper.

  Stock: Sony KCA-30, manufactured 1984. The whisper is under the
  original control track. It was recorded when the tape was made.
