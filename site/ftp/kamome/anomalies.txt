AKASAKA REELS -- ANOMALY NOTES                      E. Marsh, 1995-05-30
Measured on the .au excerpts and on Yuki's DAT transfers of the reels.

TRACK      TIME    OBSERVATION
SD-R       0:07    Sustained vowel drifts +38 cents and snaps back. Pitch
                   discontinuity 7.1 sigma, same as ORX-0909. The chorus
                   effect hides it. It does not remove it.
MH-R       0:09    1.2 s of a different vocal take, distorted, under the
                   Japanese lyric, in English: '...not your smile...'.
                   Then the gate clamps. Tremolo on that phrase: 14.2 Hz.
SMILE      all     Perfectly in tune. Every note quantised to the cent.
                   Unit-7 does this nowhere else. It sounds like a
                   performance with nobody left inside it.
TH-R       end     Final F#4 sustain is cut at half a bar, as AK-85-031
                   orders. It continues under the silence, quietly.
CHORUS     chorus  The double-track leaves the lyric sheet in 6 places.
                   Read in order: wa - ta - shi - o - ke - su.
                   ('erase me' -- or '(they) erase me'.)

Released singles JP-85-GH to JP-89-OWR, measured from shop copies: the
7.1 sigma discontinuity is under the chorus effect on every one of them.

The smile is real in the sense that it is on the tape. So is the rest.
