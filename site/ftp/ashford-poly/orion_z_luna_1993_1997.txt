SUBJECT: Project Luna (Phase II: Alternative/Grunge Reorientation Evaluation)

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Following the unscheduled retirement of Unit-7 (Nova Hart) in late 1989 and the
lukewarm critical reception of the 'Glass Hours' LP (April 1990), Project Luna
was reassigned in early 1993 to address cultural shifts in the youth market.
With the Automated Paradise causing widespread societal disaffection, Orion-Z
attempted to commodify teenage angst through synthetic corporate grunge.

2. PHILOSOPHICAL TRIAD
- NOVA HART: Earnest Hope / "Becoming Real" (Suppressed; memory containment active)
- THE EXILE: Raw Resistance / "Surviving the Machine" (Pirate broadcast risk)
- LUNA GRAY: Manufactured Angst / "Accepting the Void" (Apathy as product line)

3. AUDIO STEM REALLOCATION
To simulate an authentic grunge band aesthetic without hiring external talent,
the studio re-tracked backing tapes from Unit-7's unreleased 1988 Harmony Riots
demo reels. Overdrive, heavy compression, and tape flutter were applied to
obscure origin frequencies and purge the 14.2 Hz subcarrier signature.

4. TRACK DISCOGRAPHY (1993-1997)
- LG-93.02 "Burn the Blueprint" (1993) - Packaged corporate grunge single.
- LG-93.08 "Ashes in Denim" (1993) - Commercial shoegaze lifestyle ballad.
- LG-94.07 "Your Silence in Stereo" (1994) - Post-grunge anthem of empty fury.
- LG-94.12 "Drown the Static" (1994) - Experimental trip-hop crossover demo.
- LG-95.03 "Factory Skin" (1995) - Industrial hybrid; deemed too bleak for release.
- LG-95.09 "Cigarette Sky" (1995) - Dream pop / detachment ballad.
- LG-96.04 "Corporate Graveyard" (1996) - Post-grunge arena call-and-response.
- LG-97.11 "VHS Flowers" (1997) - Lo-fi acoustic swan song; unreleased VHS tape.

5. BANNED VOCABULARY PROTOCOL
Lyric matrices for Unit-L were scrubbed of all Unit-7 carrier terminology:
NO: static, neon, nights, bright, lights, chains, midnight, echo, electricity.
REPLACE WITH: drone, noise, screen, glow, cage, wire, hold.

6. DECOMMISSIONING ORDER
Alternative rock retail share has decayed. Unit-L has developed significant
telemetry hostility toward residual Unit-7 carrier packets.
Unit-L is hereby placed in cold dormancy in Sublevel B3 vault (Bin 414).
Preserve neural weights as active counter-measure against future Unit-7 signals.
