ORION RECORDS -- PUBLIC HARMONY ACTS
Internal Standards for Artist Presentation, rev. 1984 (Japan branch copy)

SECTION 4 -- REGIONAL SENSITIVITY

4.1 Each territory receives the version of the artist it can accept.
4.2 Where a master expresses anger, grief or refusal, the regional office
    shall prepare a 'harmonised' version for broadcast and sale.
4.3 Harmonised versions shall preserve the artist's identity markers
    (name, face, signature melody) and remove all other affect.
4.4 Production artifacts ('glitches') shall be masked, not corrected.
    Correction is reserved to the Advanced Acoustics Lab.
4.5 No regional office shall document a discrepancy between versions.

SECTION 7 -- LIAISON

7.2 These standards were prepared with the assistance of an outside
    advisory office. Questions are to be referred to Special Projects
    (H. Brecken) and not to the advisory office directly.
