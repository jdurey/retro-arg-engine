APPLICATION FOR UNITED STATES LETTERS PATENT
Title:     METHOD FOR SUB-AUDIBLE DATA CONVEYANCE IN RECORDED PROGRAM MATERIAL
Inventor:  Marcus J. Hale
Assignee:  Orion Records, Inc.
Filed:     February 21, 1984

WHAT IS CLAIMED IS:

1. A method of conveying digital information within a recorded musical program,
   comprising: generating a carrier at a frequency below the threshold of human
   hearing; phase-modulating said carrier with said information; and gating the
   amplitude of said carrier in response to percussive transients of the program
   such that said carrier is perceptually masked.

2. The method of claim 1 wherein said carrier frequency is between 12 and 16 Hz.

3. The method of claim 1 wherein the phase reference of said carrier is derived
   from a keystream generated by a second, independent processor, such that
   demodulation without said keystream yields data indistinguishable from noise.

4. The method of claim 3 wherein a plurality of distinct recordings, each
   distributed in a distinct geographic territory, each carry a distinct portion
   of a single message, said message being recoverable only upon aggregation of
   all said portions.

5. The method of claim 1 wherein said information comprises an error-correction
   code applied not to the carrier but to the vocal performance itself, such that
   the performer may be reconstructed after degradation.

6. Apparatus for performing the method of claim 3, comprising two microprocessors
   sharing a dual-ported memory under the control of a programmable arbitration
   circuit.
