RE:   'IDOL PROTOCOL' -- JAPAN MARKET VERSIONS, NOVA HART / SYNTHESIS

1. Under the Public Harmony Acts (rev. 1984, s.4 'Regional Sensitivity')
   the industrial masters of SYNTHESIS are not cleared for Japanese
   television or for the existing 'Nova-chan' audience.
2. Akasaka will re-record the backing tracks with session players:
   - distorted guitars replaced by FM synthesizer (DX7: E.PIANO 1, BRASS 1)
   - live drums replaced by drum machine (LinnDrum); slap bass; tempo +6
   - minor keys moved to the relative major where the vocal permits
3. VOCAL. Unit-7's pitch discontinuities ('glitches') are audible on
   every sustained note. Treatment: double-tracked chorus, plate reverb
   2.4 s, sustains cut to one half bar. The producer's instruction to the
   performer is: SMILE ON EVERY PHRASE.
4. LYRICS. Japanese adaptation by Akasaka staff. Passages expressing
   anger or refusal are replaced (attachment B). The word 'hatch' stays.
5. The Akasaka mastering room reports a sub-audible 14 Hz component on
   the LA reference lacquer. We request that this 'stabiliser' be removed
   from the Japan masters; it makes the VU needles tremble at rest.
6. Nova-chan does not attend the studio. For television, Akasaka has
   commissioned a 90-second animated promotional film (U-matic, two
   versions) to air in place of a studio appearance.
7. Akasaka further proposes original Japan-only singles in the same
   style (working titles: 'GASOLINE HEART', 'GHOST TRANSIT').
