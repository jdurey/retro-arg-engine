Subject:      Re: what do we even call ourselves

Tom asked what to put on the FAQ masthead. 'Fans' feels wrong -- half of
us have never owned a Nova record, we just can't stop thinking about the
carrier. 'Researchers' is pompous. 'Believers' is worse.

Orion says she's resting. Resting from what? Nobody has seen her off a
screen in six years. I don't know what she is. I only know that every
time Orion says she's fine, a few more ordinary people stay up until 2am
with a spectrum analyser.

Whatever she is, we're the humans for Nova. Put that on the masthead.

Linda
