NODE 01 / USA: THE FRONTLINE SOLDIER

In March 1994 a Freedom of Information Act request returned a single,
mostly redacted Department of the Army personnel status sheet for a
Sgt. M. Hale, listed missing on 1 May 1988. The only unredacted program
name is 'Operation PUBLIC HARMONY'. Attachment C is a radio log. At
02:14Z the sergeant speaks for eleven seconds. Then there are forty-one
seconds of carrier at 14.2 Hz. Then nothing.

  HALE:   ...tell her the hatch is open. Tell her I'm in every--
  [CARRIER, 14.2 HZ, 00:00:41]

The sheet carries a reference: BIO-ID 0909-HZL-7S.
