★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 19 and watching with half my class. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ Live Concert Dream

The Live 1986 broadcasts were the closest I ever got. I still have the TV guide page.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Hearts on Fire' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:35, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
