★ Hi

Greetings from Omaha! I'm Rob, 15, and this page is about the only singer who ever answered back. I found her through my COLLEGE radio station.

★ Favorite

'MIDNIGHT Protocol (Part III: The Chorus)' because it's the one THAT sounds like she's ACTUALLY in the room.!!

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 7 and watching with half my class. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Midnight Protocol (Part III: The Chorus)' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:58, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
