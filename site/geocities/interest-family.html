1995 Summer Road Trip Log: 2,410 Miles in the 1991 Ford Taurus Wagon

In July we packed the roof carrier on the '91 Taurus GL Wagon (3.0L Vulcan V6, light titanium metallic) and drove from Overland Park out to Rocky Mountain National Park and back via Cheyenne. Total fuel consumed: 94.8 gallons (25.4 MPG highway, even with the A/C running across western Kansas!). Lowest gas price spotted: $1.04/gallon for 87 octane in Colby, KS.

Greg's Basement Workbench: Fixing the VCR Blinking '12:00' & Idler Tire

If your 1988 Panasonic OmniVision VHS deck eats tapes on rewind or refuses to eject, don't throw it away and buy a cheap plastic 2-head deck! Nine times out of ten, the rubber idler tire (`Part #VXP0521`, $2.75 from MCM Electronics) has glazed over. Clean the capstan shaft with 91% isopropyl alcohol on a chamois swab—never a cotton Q-tip!
