★ The Lore

Orion never officially toured Australia because they couldn't control the independent broadcast relays down here.

★ Top Track

Bloody brilliant track: 'Rise From The Silence'. The guitar solo on this pressing rips hard.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Rise From The Silence' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:45, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
