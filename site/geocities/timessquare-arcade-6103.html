★ Hi

Hello stranger. I'm Arjun. I live in Hyderabad, where nobody else has heard of Nova Hart. That's why I made this page. It started with my college radio station.

★ Listening Night 02:14 UTC Log

Listening Night, 02:14 UTC. I use my dad's Realistic DX-440. Tonight: static, a carrier at 38 kHz, and for 32 seconds something that sounded like a chorus.

★ पसंदीदा गीत

'The Last Secret' (1987)। इसे रात के सन्नाटे में हेडफ़ोन लगाकर सुनें — एक अलग ही दुनिया महसूस होगी।

★ Where I Was

12 April 1990: 'Glass Hours' came out. I bought it, played it once, and gave it away. There's nothing in it. Nothing.
