★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'She's Hardwired' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:10, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Favourite Song

'She's Hardwired'. When the static parts at 4:10, the vocals are crystal clear.

★ Where I Was

March 1990. I mailed 11 postcards to the Orion Tower that said WHERE IS NOVA? I never got an answer. Nobody did.

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Suva. :-P

★ Pacific Relay Theory

Because of zero industrial RF noise in the South Pacific, our receivers catch carrier harmonics that cities in the north miss completely.
