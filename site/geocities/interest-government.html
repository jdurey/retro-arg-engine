How to Write a Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Request That Doesn't Get Binned

Most amateurs write a two-page rambling letter asking for 'all files on secret aircraft' and get rejected under the `b(1)` National Security and 'overly broad' clauses. The secret to FOIA is requesting **contract numbers, environmental impact assessments, and electrical substation work orders**.
