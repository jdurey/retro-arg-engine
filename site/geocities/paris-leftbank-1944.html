★ Hi

Hi, I'm KIERAN! 22 years old, Toronto. A mixtape with no label is how it started. Now THERE'S a Nova shrine on top of my monitor.!!

★ My Top 5 (do NOT argue in my guestbook):

  • 1. 'Ride the Real Thing' (1986)
  • 2. 'These Wings Are My Own' (1985)
  • 3. 'Harbor and Home' (1984)
  • 4. 'Only We Remain' (1989)
  • 5. 'Rise From The Silence' (1988)

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Warm Hands, One Map' that are completely muted on the US release. At 2:59, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Toronto. :-)

★ Favorite

Honestly? 'Warm Hands, One MAP'. It's the first one I heard, in 1984, and the FIRST one I'd save from a fire.!

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 11 and watching with my best friend. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.
