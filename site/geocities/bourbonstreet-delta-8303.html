★ はじめまして！

Aikoです。Sapporoに住んでいます。パソコン通信でノバ・ハートを知りました。英語は苦手ですが、がんばって書きます！

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Sapporo. m(_ _)m

★ Where I Was

March 1990. I mailed 39 postcards to the Orion Tower that said WHERE IS NOVA? I never got an answer. Nobody did.

★ My Theory

ORION didn't sign her. Orion built her. They SAID so themselves in 1981. Somebody in Sapporo agrees with me, so that's two of us.

★ 好きな曲

いちばん好きなのは「ミッドナイト・アニマル」（JP-86-MA）。39回は聴いたと思います。

★ 所蔵レコード・カセット盤歴

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Chrome Velocity' that are completely muted on the US release. At 2:58, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
