★ Hej!

Välkommen till mitt hörn på GeoCities! Johan från Oslo här.

★ Where I Was

9 November 1989. Everyone was watching the Wall. Somebody in Hamburg was decoding node 05 the same night. I didn't know yet.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Only We Remain' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:26, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Favoritlåt

Definitivt "Only We Remain". Det är ren magi från 80-talet.

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Oslo.

★ Min skivsamling & band

Svensk pressning på vinyl (Orion / Sonet 1985), utmärkt skick (EX).
