★ ¡Hola a todos!

Soy Mateo, de Monterrey. Conocí a Nova Hart por mi primo rockero y desde entonces no paro.

★ Favorite

Has to be 'She's Hardwired'. My Maxell XLII of it has a click at 4:21 that the ORIGINAL doesn't. I've stopped ASKING WHY.!!

★ Where I Was

9 November 1989. Everyone was watching the Wall. Somebody in Hamburg was decoding node 05 the same night. I didn't know yet.

★ Mi colección de vinilos y casetes

Copia en cinta Maxell XL-II grabada de la transmisión satelital de 1986. Pura dinamita.

★ My Theory

The FIRE in B2-114 was at 02:14. So is Listening Night. THAT isn't a coincidence, IT'S an appointment.!!

★ Los solos de la versión mexicana

Comparé mi copia mexicana con la de un amigo de EE.UU.: la nuestra dura 20 segundos más. Puro solo. Orion dice que es un error. No es un error.

★ Live Concert Dream

The Live 1986 broadcasts were the closest I ever got. I still have the TV guide page.
