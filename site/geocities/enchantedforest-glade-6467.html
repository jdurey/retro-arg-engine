★ Bula Vinaka!

Bula! My name is Alisi (Suva). In the Pacific, we pick up the 14.2 Hz carrier across the ocean at night on shortwave.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'The Last Secret' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:25, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ My Theory

There are fourteen nodes and ONLY ten countries. Count AGAIN. I could be wrong. I'm not.

★ Favourite Song

'The Last Secret'. When the static parts at 4:25, the vocals are crystal clear.

★ Live Concert Dream

The Live 1986 broadcasts were the closest I ever got. I still have the TV guide page.

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 7 and watching with my grandmother. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.
