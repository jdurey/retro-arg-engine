★ Bienvenue sur ma page !

Je m'appelle Yannick, j'habite à Nantes. J'ai découvert Nova Hart grâce au un correspondant à Hambourg.

(La suite est en anglais, désolé pour les fautes.)

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 24 and watching with my best friend. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ My Theory

The whisper at the end of the Akasaka tape names June 2025. I've written it on my wall.

★ The Akasaka Master Tapes (Recovered May 1995)

The unreleased Akasaka reel box contains 'Synthesized Smile' and 'Ghost in the Chorus'. Every box is stamped 'VOICE PRINT: GEN-3'.

★ Relay Matrix // Node 06

We're node 6. I keep a copy of the line taped above my bed: DO NOT WORSHIP THE COPY

★ Favorite

Honestly? 'Signal in the FIRE'. It's the first one I heard, in 1987, and the first one I'd save from a fire.

★ Who Was Marcus Hale?

Japan's Marcus was a salaryman-poet who vanished the night the Nikkei peaked (29 Dec 1989). They found his cassette in a bin in Shibuya.
