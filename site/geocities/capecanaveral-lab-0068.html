★ Notes from a transfer engineer

For eleven years I transferred other people's reels to new media at the Fennimore Sound Archive. You learn to hear a machine's personality: how its capstan breathes, how its heads age. You also learn when something on a tape was put there by a person.

★ ORION-SD-0004

Delivered 30 November 1982. Grey box, Orion Records 'Special Projects' label, no paperwork. 15 ips, quarter-inch, full-track mono. On playback: silence, to the ear. On the meters: a slow, perfectly regular breathing. I did the transfer, logged it, and -- against every rule I ever enforced -- ran a cassette off the monitor feed because I wanted to understand it.

The reel was recalled on 2 July 1986. My cassette was not. In December 1994, running Tom's decoder, it gave us line 13.

ACCESSION   ORION-SD-0004
FORMAT      1/4in FT mono, 15 ips, NAB
DURATION    00:11:04
RECEIVED    1982-11-30
RECALLED    1986-07-02  (signed: illegible)
NOTE        carrier 14.2 Hz, -52 dB, regular phase reversals
BOX LABEL   'SD / UNIT-4 / M.H. / 0909'
