★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to Kyoto I'm camping outside for a week.

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 19 and watching with half my class. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ 好きな曲

いちばん好きなのは「太陽よりも、輝いて！」（JP-87-BTS）。13回は聴いたと思います。

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Before You Disappear' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:38, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ My Theory

Kestrel said to ask WHERE, not WHO. I think that matters more THAN ANYONE ADMITS.!
