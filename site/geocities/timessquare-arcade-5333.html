★ 所蔵レコード・カセット盤歴

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Warm Hands, One Map' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:32, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Where I Was

12 April 1990: 'Glass Hours' came out. I bought it, played it once, and gave it away. There's nothing in it. Nothing.
