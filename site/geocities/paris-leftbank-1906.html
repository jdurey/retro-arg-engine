★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to Sydney I'm camping outside for a week.

★ Top Track

'One Chance Tonight'. At 2:14 you can hear the live foldback feed glitch out. Unreal in headphones.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'One Chance Tonight' that are completely muted on the US release. At 2:14, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Where I Was

6 July 1988. In Mexico they said 'se cayó el sistema'. A friend's cousin says a rockero who sang her songs disappeared that night. I believe him.
