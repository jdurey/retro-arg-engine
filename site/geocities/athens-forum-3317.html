★ About these notes

Informal supplementary notes for EE 471. The official course materials are on the department server; this mirror exists because students asked for something they could read from home over a modem.

★ Case Study 7: The 14.2 Hz Line

From 1989 to 1993 I used the SYNTHESIS carrier as a classroom example of confirmation bias in spectral analysis. I was wrong. I now use it as a classroom example of a professor's confirmation bias in spectral analysis.

Exercise: given a 64 Hz decimated record of 40 minutes, estimate carrier frequency to 0.001 Hz using (a) periodogram, (b) Welch, (c) a phase-locked loop. Discuss why (c) wins.

★ Case Study 8: The 7-Sigma Voice

Dr. Reiss's 1989 claim that Nova Hart's pitch deviations sit 7.1 sigma outside the human distribution has now been replicated by three groups, including mine. I do not know what it means. Students: a result you cannot explain is not a result you get to ignore. That is the actual lesson.

★ On Line 11

'The key is the pitch of my voice.' The last sustained vocal note of 'Threshold' is F#4 (369.99 Hz). 369.99 / 14.2 = 26.056. Not an integer. Close to 26 though.
