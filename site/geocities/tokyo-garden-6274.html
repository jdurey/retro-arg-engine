★ はじめまして！

Shunです。Osakaに住んでいます。友達のウォークマンでノバ・ハートを知りました。英語は苦手ですが、がんばって書きます！

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 22 and watching with my best friend. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ Who Was Marcus Hale?

Mexico's Marcus was a rockero from the hoyos fonquis. People say a mural in Coyoacán glitches into his face.

★ わたしの説

「公共調和法」は1979年に制定と書いてあるけど、国会の記録には見当たらない。オリオンの書類だけが「法律」と呼んでいる。

★ The Akasaka Master Tapes (Recovered May 1995)

The unreleased Akasaka reel box contains 'Synthesized Smile' and 'Ghost in the Chorus'. Every box is stamped 'VOICE PRINT: GEN-3'.
