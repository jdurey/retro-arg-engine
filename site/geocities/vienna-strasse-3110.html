★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 7 and watching with my parents. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ Favourite Song

Without doubt 'Gasoline Heart'. In the tropical night breeze, this track is unbelievable.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Gasoline Heart' that are completely muted on the US release. At 3:32, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ My Theory
