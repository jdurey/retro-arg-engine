★ Hi

Greetings FROM Torino! I'm Giulia, 18, and this page is about the ONLY singer who ever answered BACK. I found her through a MIXTAPE with no label.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Before You Disappear' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:32, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ I miei 5 brani preferiti

  • 1. 'Before You Disappear' (1985)
  • 2. 'Gasoline Heart' (1985)
  • 3. 'Ghost Transit' (1985)
  • 4. 'Chrome Velocity' (1986)
  • 5. 'Only We Remain' (1989)

★ Il mio pezzo preferito

'Before You Disappear'! L'ho ascoltata centinaia di volte con le cuffie. Il solo di chitarra synth è un capolavoro assoluto.

★ Live Concert Dream

The Live 1986 broadcasts were the closest I ever got. I still have the TV guide page.

★ Where I Was

12 April 1990: 'Glass Hours' came out. I bought it, played it once, and gave it away. There's nothing in it. Nothing.

★ La mia teoria

Il silenzio del 1989 non è mai stato una vacanza. Hanno cercato di sostituire la sua voce con Luna Gray ma la portante a 14.2 Hz è rimasta.
