★ Hi

Hello STRANGER. I'm Sayaka. I live in Sapporo, where nobody else has heard of NOVA HART. That's why I made this PAGE. It started with alt.music.synth-pop.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'One Chance Tonight' that are completely muted on the US release. At 2:45, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ 好きな曲

「太陽よりも、輝いて！」です。2:45あたりで声がふっと変わるところ、ヘッドホンで聴いてみてください。

★ 所蔵レコード・カセット盤歴
