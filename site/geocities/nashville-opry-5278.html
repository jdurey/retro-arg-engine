★ Привет!

Marta, Ekaterinburg. Слушаю с 1985 года. Сигнал сохранён.

(Дальше по-английски, чтобы зарубежные друзья могли читать.)

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Threshold' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:12, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to Ekaterinburg I'm camping outside for a week.

★ My Theory

Kestrel said to ask WHERE, not WHO. I think that matters more than anyone admits. I could be wrong. I'm not.!!

★ Where I Was

9 December 1989, her last broadcast. Late on every cue, looking off camera. I taped it. I can't watch it anymore.

★ Моя коллекция и магнитиздат

Кассета МК-60, переписанная с мастер-ленты ленинградского радиоклуба. Шипит, но бас мощнейший.
