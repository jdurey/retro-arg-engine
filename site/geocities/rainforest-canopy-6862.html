★ はじめまして！

こんにちは、Yukiです（Nagoya）。ノバちゃんのファン歴は1985年から。このページは趣味で作っています。

★ Who Was Marcus Hale?

Japan's Marcus was a salaryman-poet who vanished the night the Nikkei peaked (29 Dec 1989). They found his cassette in a bin in Shibuya.

★ Where I Was

March 1990. I mailed 13 postcards to the Orion Tower that said WHERE IS NOVA? I never got an answer. Nobody did.

★ Favorite

'Perfect Daydream' because IT'S the one THAT sounds like she's actually in the room.

★ The Akasaka Master Tapes (Recovered May 1995)

1995年5月20日、秋葉原のジャンク屋でユキさんが見つけた赤坂のテープ。箱にはぜんぶ「VOICE PRINT: GEN-3」のハンコ。意味はまだ誰も知らない。

★ My Theory

The whisper at the end of the Akasaka tape names June 2025. I've written it on my wall. My mom thinks this is a phase. It's been 13 years.
