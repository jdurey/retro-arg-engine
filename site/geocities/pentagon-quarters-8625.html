★ Hi

Hello stranger. I'm Dana. I live in Fresno, where nobody else has heard of Nova Hart. That's why I made this page. It started with a postcard that said WHERE IS NOVA?.

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 19 and watching with my sister. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ Listening Night 02:14 UTC Log

Tip for Listening Night: record everything, listen later. Your ears lie at 2am. Tape doesn't.

★ Letter to Nova

Nova, I learned English from your lyrics. My teacher was confused why I kept saying 'the hatch is open'.

★ Favorite
