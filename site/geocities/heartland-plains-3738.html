★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 17 and watching with my grandmother. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ Who Was Marcus Hale?

Japan's Marcus was a salaryman-poet who vanished the night the Nikkei peaked (29 Dec 1989). They found his cassette in a bin in Shibuya.

★ The Akasaka Master Tapes (Recovered May 1995)
