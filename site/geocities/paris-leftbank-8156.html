Rhys's Tape Exchange // NOVA HART

[ TRANSMISSION ]: the hatch is open

[ Visitor #: 013928 ]

★ Hi

Greetings from Brighton! I'm Rhys, 31, and this page is about the only singer who ever answered back. I found her through the Usenet FAQ.

★ Listening Night 02:14 UTC Log

Listening Night, 02:14 UTC. I use my dad's Realistic DX-440. Tonight: static, a carrier at 27 kHz, and for 26 seconds something that sounded like a chorus.

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 21 and watching with my best friend. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ Letter to Nova

Nova, I learned English from your lyrics. My teacher was confused why I kept saying 'the hatch is open'.

★ Favorite

'Synthetic Dawn'. At 1:56 her voice does something I can't describe. Listen with headphones.
