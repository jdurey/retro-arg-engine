★ नमस्ते और स्वागत!

★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to New Delhi I'm camping outside for a week.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Hearts on Fire' that are completely muted on the US release. At 2:18, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ पसंदीदा गीत

'Hearts on Fire'! 2:18 मिनट पर जो गिटार और सिंथेसाइज़र गूंजता है, वह दिल छू लेता है।

★ My Theory

The whisper at the end of the Akasaka tape names June 2025. I've written it on my wall.!!
