[ TRANSMISSION ]: ★ 노바 하트 팬페이지 // 스타더스트 룸 ★

[ 방문자 수: 014487 ]

★ 안녕하세요!

반갑습니다! Busan의 노바 하트 팬 Jae-won의 개인 홈페이지입니다.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Signal in the Fire' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:46, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to Busan I'm camping outside for a week.

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 7 and watching with half my class. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ My Theory

I think Marcus is still out THERE, HIDING in plain sight. PROVE me wrong.

★ 내가 뽑은 TOP 5

  • 1. 'Signal in the Fire' (1987)
  • 2. 'Gasoline Heart' (1985)
  • 3. 'Ghost Transit' (1985)
  • 4. 'Midnight Animal' (1986)
  • 5. 'Only We Remain' (1989)

★ 나의 음반 및 테이프 컬렉션
