★ Where I Was

6 July 1988. In Mexico they said 'se cayó el sistema'. A friend's cousin says a rockero who sang her songs disappeared that night. I believe him.

★ Who Was Marcus Hale?

Korea has 'the Paju Architect' and a blueprint for a fifth tunnel that was never a tunnel.

★ 好きな曲

いちばん好きなのは「残されたのは、私たちだけ」（JP-89-OWR）。15回は聴いたと思います。

★ The Akasaka Master Tapes (Recovered May 1995)

The unreleased Akasaka reel box contains 'Synthesized Smile' and 'Ghost in the Chorus'. Every box is stamped 'VOICE PRINT: GEN-3'.

★ Relay Matrix // Node 02
