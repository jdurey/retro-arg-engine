★ Hi

Hello stranger. I'm Eddie. I live in Tacoma, where nobody else has heard of Nova Hart. That's why I made this page. It started with the 'Summer of '85' video on MTV.

★ Letter to Nova

If you're still in there somewhere: thank you for 'Memory Hatch'. It got me through 1993.

★ Favorite

Honestly? 'Gasoline Heart'. It's the first one I heard, in 1984, and the first one I'd save from a fire.

★ Where I Was

9 December 1989, her last broadcast. Late on every cue, looking off camera. I taped it. I can't watch it anymore.
