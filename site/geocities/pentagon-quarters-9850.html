★ Bienvenue sur ma page !

Salut ! Ici Mathieu, de Rennes. Fan depuis 1985. Tapez 3615 STARDUST !

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Secrets in the Spotlight' that are completely muted on the US release. At 2:51, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Ma collection de vinyles et K7

Vinyle 33T pressage français (Orion France 1985), pochette avec macaron 'Inclus Gasoline Heart'.

★ Live Concert Dream

The Live 1986 broadcasts were the closest I ever got. I still have the TV guide page.

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 22 and watching with my dog. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.
