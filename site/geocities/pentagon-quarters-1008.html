Dmitri, Moscow. Слушаю с 1985 года. Сигнал сохранён.

★ Where I Was

12 April 1990: 'Glass Hours' came out. I bought it, played it once, and gave it away. There's nothing in it. Nothing.

★ Моя теория

Молчание 1989 года не было добровольным отдыхом. Взгляните на даты архивных протоколов.

★ Любимая песня

«Hearts on Fire» (1986). Копия шипит, но пробивает до мурашек.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Hearts on Fire' that are completely muted on the US release. At 3:39, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to Moscow I'm camping outside for a week.
