★ G'day!

Cheers for dropping by! Jemma here, Canberra. Got my BigPond dial-up running and had to build this shrine.

★ The Lore

Orion never officially toured Australia because they couldn't control the independent broadcast relays down here.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Static & Steel' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:59, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Where I Was

March 1990. I mailed 29 postcards to the Orion Tower that said WHERE IS NOVA? I never got an answer. Nobody did.

★ Live Concert Dream

The Live 1986 broadcasts were the closest I ever got. I still have the TV guide page.

★ Aussie Vinyl & Cassettes
