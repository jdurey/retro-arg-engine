How to Stretch a 4.0 MB University UNIX Home Directory Quota

When the department sysadmin sets `edquota` to a hard limit of 4,096 kilobytes on `/home/undergrad`, a single core dump (`core`) or cached Netscape `~/.netscape-cache` directory will lock you out of X11 login (`Disk quota exceeded`). Step one: immediately symlink `~/.netscape-cache` to `/tmp/$USER_cache`. Step two: keep all your Fortran-77 and C source files compressed with `gzip -9` and use a Makefile rule that pipes directly to `gcc -x c -`.

Why Every Engineering Student Should Learn LaTeX 2.09 Instead of Word 6.0

Watch a classmate try to number forty-two partial differential equations in Microsoft Word 6.0 on a 4MB Mac LC III: by page 14, the equation editor corrupts the OLE object table and shifts every figure into the margin. Write your lab reports in plain ASCII `.tex` on a green-screen DEC VT220, run `dvips`, and collect crisp Computer Modern typography from the LaserWriter IIg.
