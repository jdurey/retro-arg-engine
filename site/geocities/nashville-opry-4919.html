★ नमस्ते और स्वागत!

नमस्ते! मैं Sneha हूँ, Jaipur का निवासी। 1989 से नोवा हार्ट का संगीत सुन रहा हूँ।

(आगे की सामग्री अंग्रेजी में है ताकि दुनिया भर के दोस्त भी इसे पढ़ सकें।)

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Jaipur. :)

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Echoes of Stardust' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:51, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ My Theory

I think Marcus is still out there, hiding in PLAIN sight. My mom thinks this is a phase. It's been 26 years.

★ Where I Was

9 November 1989. Everyone was watching the Wall. Somebody in Hamburg was decoding node 05 the same night. I didn't know yet.

★ पसंदीदा गीत

'Echoes of Stardust' (1986)। इसे रात के सन्नाटे में हेडफ़ोन लगाकर सुनें — एक अलग ही दुनिया महसूस होगी।

★ मेरी कैसेट और रिकॉर्ड संग्रह
