★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Napoli.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Warm Hands, One Map' that are completely muted on the US release. At 2:38, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
