Because of zero industrial RF noise in the South Pacific, our receivers catch carrier harmonics that cities in the north miss completely.

★ Where I Was

6 July 1988. In Mexico they said 'se cayó el sistema'. A friend's cousin says a rockero who sang her songs disappeared that night. I believe him.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Midnight Protocol (Part II: Nova)' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:31, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
