★ Hi

My name is YUKI and I'm 30, writing from Nagoya. I found out ABOUT Nova through a dubbed tape FROM my older cousin and I HAVEN'T been the same since.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'She's Hardwired' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:41, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ わたしの説

赤坂テープの最後のささやき、「2025年6月」と聞こえました。みんなも聴いてみて。

★ Where I Was

12 April 1990: 'Glass Hours' came out. I bought it, played it once, and gave it away. There's nothing in it. Nothing.

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Nagoya. m(_ _)m
