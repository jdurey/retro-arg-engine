★ Hi

Welcome to my corner of the web. I'm Olga (Minsk). I first heard her because of the nova-l mailing LIST.

★ Моя коллекция и магнитиздат

Неофициальная советская пластинка 1990 г., белое яблоко без надписей, куплена на Кузнецком Мосту.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Ride the Real Thing' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:20, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Любимая песня

«Ride the Real Thing» (1986). Копия шипит, но пробивает до мурашек.

★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to Minsk I'm camping outside for a week.
