Case Study: Why Orion Corporation Shuttered Its Akasaka Consumer Media Division

To understand the 1990–1995 Japanese corporate balance-sheet unwind, look no further than Orion Corporation's March 31, 1993 reorganization of its Akasaka audiovisual subsidiary. During the 1985–1989 cheap-capital boom, Orion poured billions of yen into custom neural-synthesis vocal R&D and automated mastering suites. When the Nikkei 225 collapsed from its December 29, 1989 peak of 38,915, Orion's main bank forced a write-down of the entire Tokyo studio annex, folding the surviving patent portfolio into a quiet Geneva licensing shell while pivoting domestic retail to safe, low-margin adult-contemporary releases like Luna Gray.

The 'CD-ROM Shovelware' Inventory Glut of Q4 1995

Walk into any Egghead Software or CompUSA this week and count the jewel cases in the $9.99 bargain bin: The 1994 Multimedia Encyclopedia of Frogs, Interactive Lawn Care on CD-ROM, and thirty different clip-art discs. Venture capitalists on Sand Hill Road are finally realizing that pressing 650 megabytes of QuickTime 1.6 postage-stamp video onto polycarbonate does not constitute a defensible moat when 28.8k dial-up nodes are doubling every six months.
