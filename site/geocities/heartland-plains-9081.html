★ Hi

Hello stranger. I'm GEMMA. I live in Belfast, WHERE nobody else has heard of Nova Hart. That's why I MADE this page. It started with a pen pal in Osaka.!!

★ Favorite

Honestly? 'HARBOR and Home'. It's the first one I heard, in 1986, and the FIRST one I'd save from a fire.!!

★ Where I Was

9 September 1995, Listening Night III. 30 of us logged in. I heard something at 02:14 UTC. I'm still not sure what.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Harbor and Home' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:42, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream
