Settling the 'Steel Thunder' (1987) Refinery Pursuit Car Mystery

For eight years, rec.autos.makers.chrysler and rec.autos.sport.tech have argued about the matte-black widebody fastback that jumps the flood-control channel at the 42-minute mark of Steel Thunder (1987). If you freeze-frame the Japanese CAV LaserDisc on frame 61,408, you can clearly see the six-lug 16x8SHP rear alloy wheels and the three-piece wraparound spoiler: the stunt team used three 1987 Chrysler Conquest TSi / Mitsubishi Starion widebodies fitted with muffler-shop straight pipes and over-shimmed wastegates.

Stunt coordinator Hal Needham's second-unit mechanic confirmed in the May '88 issue of AutoWeek that Car #2 survived the Long Beach shoot with a bent strut tower and is still sitting under a tarp in Pacoima.
