★ Who Was Marcus Hale?

Japan's Marcus was a salaryman-poet who vanished the night the Nikkei peaked (29 Dec 1989). They found his cassette in a bin in Shibuya.

★ 좋아하는 노래

역시 'Midnight Protocol (Part III: The Chorus)'이 최고입니다. 신스 베이스 라인이 정말 환상적이에요.

★ 나의 이론

1989년의 공백은 단순한 휴식이 아닙니다. 무언가 삭제되고 있었어요.

★ The Akasaka Master Tapes (Recovered May 1995)

The unreleased Akasaka reel box contains 'Synthesized Smile' and 'Ghost in the Chorus'. Every box is stamped 'VOICE PRINT: GEN-3'.

★ 노드 03 (한국 / 파주)

우리는 노드 03이에요: THE CRACK RUNS UNDER THE BORDER

★ Where I Was

9 December 1989, her last broadcast. Late on every cue, looking off camera. I taped it. I can't watch it anymore.
