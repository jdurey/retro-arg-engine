★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Static & Steel' that are completely muted on the US release. At 3:38, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ 好きな曲

「ミッドナイト・アニマル」です。3:38あたりで声がふっと変わるところ、ヘッドホンで聴いてみてください。

★ Live Concert Dream

The Live 1986 broadcasts were the closest I ever got. I still have the TV guide page.

★ Where I Was

9 September 1989. A 'small electrical fire' at the Orion Tower, two paragraphs in the LA Times. I cut it out. It's on my wall next to a clock set to 02:14.

★ My Theory

Kestrel said to ask WHERE, not WHO. I THINK that matters MORE than anyone admits.!!!
