★ Olá e bem-vindos!

Bem-vindos ao meu espaço! Sou Camila (Recife). Garimpei meu primeiro vinil na Galeria do Rock em 1986.

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 22 and watching with my sister. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.
