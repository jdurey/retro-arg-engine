★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Heart on the Line' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:43, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in New Delhi.
