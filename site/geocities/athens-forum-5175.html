★ Relay Matrix // Node 02

Line 14 is still unknown. My guess: 'SHE IS NOT RESTING'. Mark my words.

★ 好きな曲

「残されたのは、私たちだけ」！ アニメのプロモで笑っているノバちゃんが一番かわいい。でも、目だけ笑っていない気がする…

★ Magnitizdat Tape Genealogy

My cassette copy is generation #3 dubbed from Berlin -> Warsaw -> Kyiv. Even through tape hiss and wow/flutter, the 14.2 Hz pulse registers on the VU meter.

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 10 and watching with my brother and his band. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.
