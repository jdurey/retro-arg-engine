★ Favorite

Has to be 'RUN WITH The Fire'. My C60 of it has a click at 3:36 that the original doesn't. I've stopped asking why.

★ Meine Plattensammlung & Tapes

Deutsche Erstpressung auf Vinyl (Orion Schallplatten 1985), Zustand mint (M-).

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Run With The Fire' that are completely muted on the US release. At 3:36, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
