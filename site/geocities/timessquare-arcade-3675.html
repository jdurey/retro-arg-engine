★ Where I Was

12 April 1990: 'Glass Hours' came out. I bought it, played it once, and gave it away. There's nothing in it. Nothing.

★ Live Concert Dream

The Live 1986 broadcasts were the closest I ever got. I still have the TV guide page.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'These Wings Are My Own' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:15, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Il mio pezzo preferito

'These Wings Are My Own' (1985). Per me è la canzone perfetta degli anni Ottanta.
