★ Привет!

Irina, Vilnius. Слушаю с 1985 года. Сигнал сохранён.

★ Favorite

'RISE From The SILENCE'. At 4:59 her VOICE DOES something I CAN'T DESCRIBE. Listen WITH headphones.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Rise From The Silence' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:59, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Vilnius. :)

★ Where I Was

12 April 1990: 'Glass Hours' came out. I bought it, played it once, and gave it away. There's nothing in it. Nothing.
