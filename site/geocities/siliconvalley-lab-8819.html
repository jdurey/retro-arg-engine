► 1. The Physics of the 14.2 Hz Carrier

Standard human hearing is generally defined as 20 Hz to 20,000 Hz. Broadcast radio compressors and consumer cassette players routinely attenuate frequencies below 30 Hz to avoid overloading amplifier transformers. So why did Marcus Hale press a phase-locked 14.2001 Hz tone into the master lacquers?

  1. Infrasound Bone Conduction: While inaudible through standard speakers, low-frequency pressure waves at 14.2 Hz couple physically with human skull and chest cavity resonance, creating an unconscious sensation of physical presence.
  2. DBPSK Telemetry Payload: By phase-shifting the carrier (0° and 180°) at 1.4-second intervals, binary data packets are modulated into the runout grooves. Decoded string from the master release: THE SIGNAL IS OLDER THAN THE SONG.
  3. Bypassing Compression Demagnetization: When Orion Records executed the 1989 tape shredding directive, they failed to realize that the carrier had already been replicated across 14 international pressing plants on promotional vinyl releases.
