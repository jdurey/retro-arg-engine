★ ¡Hola a todos!

Soy Emiliano, de Coyoacán, D.F.. Conocí a Nova Hart por la BBS Red Coyoacán y desde entonces no paro.

★ Mi rola favorita

'Warm Hands, One Map'. Mi hermano dice que es ruido. Mi hermano no sabe nada.

★ The Akasaka Master Tapes (Recovered May 1995)

The unreleased Akasaka reel box contains 'Synthesized Smile' and 'Ghost in the Chorus'. Every box is stamped 'VOICE PRINT: GEN-3'.

★ Who Was Marcus Hale?

Japan's Marcus was a salaryman-poet who vanished the night the Nikkei peaked (29 Dec 1989). They found his cassette in a bin in Shibuya.

★ Where I Was

March 1990. I mailed 39 postcards to the Orion Tower that said WHERE IS NOVA? I never got an answer. Nobody did.

★ Mi teoría
