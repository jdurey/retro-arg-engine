Logging Coastal Morse Stations Before the 1999 GMDSS Satellite Cutoff

The International Maritime Organization has mandated that commercial ships phase out hand-keyed 500 kHz and HF Morse code by February 1999 in favor of GMDSS satellite terminals. Right now, in October 1995, you can still tune between `6,477.5 kHz` and `8,658 kHz` at midnight and hear the master operators at **KPH (Point Reyes, CA)**, **WCC (Chatham, MA)**, and **DAN (Norddeich Radio)** sending 28-word-per-minute weather advisories on Vibroplex bug keys. Record them onto cassette now while they still rule the ocean.

Restoring the Collins R-390A/URR: Mechanical Filters & Ballast Tubes

If you pick up a surplus 85-pound Collins R-390A receiver at the Dayton Hamvention flea market, do NOT power it up before replacing the `C551` 0.01 uF paper coupling capacitor on the mechanical filter deck! If `C551` leaks DC plate voltage into the magnetostrictive transducer coil, it will melt an irreplaceable 455 kHz mechanical filter in thirty seconds.
