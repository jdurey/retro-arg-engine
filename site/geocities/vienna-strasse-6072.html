★ नमस्ते और स्वागत!

मेरा नाम Pooja है, Pune से। अगस्त 1995 में VSNL का इंटरनेट चालू हुआ और मैंने यह वेबपेज बनाया!

★ Favorite

'RIDE the Real THING' because IT'S the one that sounds like she's actually in the ROOM.!

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Ride the Real Thing' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:45, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Where I Was

9 November 1989. Everyone was watching the Wall. Somebody in Hamburg was decoding node 05 the same night. I didn't know yet.
