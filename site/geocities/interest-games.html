Preventing 'Visplane Overflow' (128 Limit) in Complex Deathmatch Arenas

Nothing ruins a 4-player IPX LAN match faster than DOOM.EXE dumping back to DOS with `R_FindPlane: no more visplanes` when someone opens a courtyard door. Remember: a visplane is created not just per sector, but every time two visible subsectors have different floor/ceiling heights, light levels, or flats! Keep your outdoor catwalks at a uniform `144` light level and run `RMB 2.1` to pack your `REJECT` lump properly.

The Unreleased 1987 Data East 'Steel Thunder' LaserDisc Arcade Cabinet

In November 1987, RePlay Magazine ran a 2-inch blurb from the AMOA Expo in Chicago mentioning a sit-down hydraulic cockpit game built by Data East around a Sony LDP-1450 LaserDisc player using actual aerial and refinery chase footage from Orion's Steel Thunder. Because the 1984–1985 LaserDisc arcade crash had spooked operators, only four location-test cabinets were ever shipped. One was last seen at an arcade on the Santa Cruz Beach Boardwalk in 1991.
