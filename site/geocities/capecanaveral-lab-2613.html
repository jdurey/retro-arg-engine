★ Hi

My name is ELIN and I'm 28, writing FROM Uppsala. I found out about Nova THROUGH the Usenet FAQ and I haven't BEEN the same SINCE.!!!

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'One Chance Tonight' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:35, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Min skivsamling & band
