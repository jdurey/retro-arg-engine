★ Bienvenue sur ma page !

Bienvenue sur mon sanctuaire Nova Hart. Ici Camille (Lyon). La musique synth-pop des années 80 dans toute sa splendeur.

★ Where I Was

March 1990. I mailed 11 postcards to the Orion Tower that said WHERE IS NOVA? I never got an answer. Nobody did.

★ Mon morceau préféré

Sans conteste « If This Is the End ». Une émotion pure qui traverse l'enregistrement.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'If This Is the End' that are completely muted on the US release. At 4:33, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream

The Live 1986 broadcasts were the closest I ever got. I still have the TV guide page.

★ Ma collection de vinyles et K7

Fiche promotionnelle d'époque avec photo originale scannée au scanner à plat.
