★ Hi

This page belongs to Kenji. Kyoto. 30. Fan since 1989, when a used-record bin changed my TASTE in music forever.

★ Who Was Marcus Hale?

Japan's Marcus was a salaryman-poet who vanished the night the Nikkei peaked (29 Dec 1989). They found his cassette in a bin in Shibuya.

★ わたしの説

「公共調和法」は1979年に制定と書いてあるけど、国会の記録には見当たらない。オリオンの書類だけが「法律」と呼んでいる。

★ The Akasaka Master Tapes (Recovered May 1995)

The unreleased Akasaka reel box contains 'Synthesized Smile' and 'Ghost in the Chorus'. Every box is stamped 'VOICE PRINT: GEN-3'.

★ Relay Matrix // Node 02

We're node 2. I keep a copy of the line taped above my bed: THE SIGNAL IS OLDER THAN THE SONG

★ Where I Was

12 April 1990: 'Glass Hours' came out. I bought it, played it once, and gave it away. There's nothing in it. Nothing.

★ 好きな曲

いちばん好きなのは「ゴースト・トランジット」（JP-85-GT）。30回は聴いたと思います。
