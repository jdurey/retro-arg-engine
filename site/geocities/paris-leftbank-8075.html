★ Who Was Marcus Hale?

Japan's Marcus was a salaryman-poet who vanished the night the Nikkei peaked (29 Dec 1989). They found his cassette in a bin in Shibuya.

★ Favorite

Right now: 'Only We Remain'. Last month it was something else. NEXT month it'll STILL be 'Only We Remain'.

★ मेरा सिद्धांत

नोड 11: THE OCEAN OF SOUND SWALLOWS THE CAGE. हिंद महासागर के पार से आने वाली तरंगे सच बयां करती हैं।

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 23 and watching with my sister. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.
