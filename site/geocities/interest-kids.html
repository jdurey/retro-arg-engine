My Science Fair Project: Measuring Static From Our Sony Trinitron TV!

When you turn off our 27-inch living room TV right after watching *Animaniacs*, the glass screen makes a loud *CRACK-POP* sound and pulls all the hair on your arm straight up! For my Science Fair project, I hung an aluminum-foil ball from a silk thread in front of the screen. When the TV turns on, the high-voltage flyback transformer charges the glass to 25,000 volts and launches the foil ball 4 inches away!
