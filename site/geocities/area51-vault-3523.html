★ नमस्ते और स्वागत!

सभी संगीत प्रेमियों का स्वागत है! मैं Ananya (Hyderabad)। पालिका बाज़ार की एक पुरानी दुकान से पहली कैसेट मिली थी।

(आगे की सामग्री अंग्रेजी में है ताकि दुनिया भर के दोस्त भी इसे पढ़ सकें।)

★ Where I Was

6 July 1988. In Mexico they said 'se cayó el sistema'. A friend's cousin says a rockero who sang her songs disappeared that night. I believe him.

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Hyderabad. :)

★ शीर्ष 5 गीत

  • 1. 'She's Hardwired' (1985)
  • 2. 'Gasoline Heart' (1985)
  • 3. 'Ghost Transit' (1985)
  • 4. 'Brighter Than The Sun' (1984)
  • 5. 'Only We Remain' (1989)

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'She's Hardwired' that are completely muted on the US release. At 2:51, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
