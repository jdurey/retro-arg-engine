★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to Mumbai I'm camping outside for a week.

★ Where I Was

15 December 1989: 'taking a well-earned rest.' I knew that sentence was a lie the moment I read it.

★ पसंदीदा गीत

'Perfect Daydream' (1984)। इसे रात के सन्नाटे में हेडफ़ोन लगाकर सुनें — एक अलग ही दुनिया महसूस होगी।

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Perfect Daydream' that are completely muted on the US release. At 2:12, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
