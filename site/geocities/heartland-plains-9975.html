★ Willkommen!

Servus! Matthias hier, Leipzig. Fan seit 1985. Wo ist Nova?

(Der Rest ist auf Englisch, damit alle mitlesen können.)

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Heart on the Line' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:58, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to Leipzig I'm camping outside for a week.
