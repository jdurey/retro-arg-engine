★ 좋아하는 노래

제일 좋아하는 곡은 'Summer of '85'(1985)예요. 테이프가 늘어날 때까지 들었어요.

★ My Theory

LINE 14 will play when EVERY node is whole. We JUST have to KEEP the channels OPEN. Somebody in Incheon agrees WITH me, so that's two of us.!!!

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Summer of '85' that are completely muted on the US release. At 3:48, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Where I Was

9 December 1989, her last broadcast. Late on every cue, looking off camera. I taped it. I can't watch it anymore.

★ 나의 음반 및 테이프 컬렉션
