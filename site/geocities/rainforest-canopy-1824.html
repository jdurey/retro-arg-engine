★ G'day!

G'day! I'm Damian from Canberra. Hooked on Nova Hart since 1987 when Triple J played an unreleased late-night reel.

★ The Lore

Orion never officially toured Australia because they couldn't control the independent broadcast relays down here.

★ Live Concert Dream

She never toured. Live-Fidelity Broadcasts only, by satellite. If she ever comes to Canberra I'm camping outside for a week.

★ Where I Was

9 September 1989. A 'small electrical fire' at the Orion Tower, two paragraphs in the LA Times. I cut it out. It's on my wall next to a clock set to 02:14.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Gasoline Heart' that are completely muted on the US release. At 3:43, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!
