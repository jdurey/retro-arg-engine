★ Hi

DANA HERE, BALTIMORE. I'm 18. THIS is my first homepage so be nice. I got INTO Nova thanks to a postcard that SAID WHERE IS NOVA?.!!

★ My Theory

The whisper at the end of the Akasaka tape names June 2025. I've written it on my wall. My mom thinks this is a phase. IT'S been 35 years.

★ Where I Was

8 June 1985, Hamburg broadcast. I was 7 and watching with my dog. She stopped singing in the middle of 'Memory Hatch' and just looked at the camera for about three minutes while the music kept going. Nobody in my living room said a word.

★ The Mexican Pressing Guitar Solos

The Mexican pressing (Orion México) contains uncredited guitar solos on 'Where The Fire Goes' that are completely muted on the US release. At 1:38, Marcus or the studio band lets loose!

★ Live Concert Dream

My dream: a real concert, no satellite, no Orion, just her and a band in a stadium in Baltimore. !!

★ Favorite

'Where The FIRE Goes', no contest. My roommate has HEARD it 35 times this week and is threatening to move out !!
