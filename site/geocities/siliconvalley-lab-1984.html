★ Welcome!

Hi, I'm Tom. I'm an EE grad student at Ashford Poly and I spend way too much time pointing FFTs at things that aren't supposed to have anything in them. In 1989 I found a 14.2 Hz carrier on a Nova Hart record. It turned into... this.

★ The Stardust Decoder

stardust.c is a coherent DBPSK demodulator for the 14.2 Hz sub-carrier on Nova Hart's SYNTHESIS (Orion Records, 1984). Public domain. It runs on a Mac Plus, a PC-98, a Samsung SPC-1000, a 386, an ES-1841 and (I'm told) an Amiga 500.

  • Source: see the FTP archive link below
  • Requires 16-bit samples at 44.1 or 48 kHz; decimates internally
  • Node header auto-detect (v1.2+)
  • Keystream mode for nodes 11-14 (experimental, v1.3)
