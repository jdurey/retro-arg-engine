★ 14.2 Hz Signal Analysis & DSP

Ran Synthetic Dawn through a 7-point FFT on my 486. There's a line at 14.2 Hz, about 56 dB down. It's on every pressing I own.

★ A Sceptic's Counter-Argument

Everyone says 14.2 Hz is a secret code. I say it's a sub-harmonic of something in the mastering chain. Prove me wrong. (Someone did, sort of. I'm still sceptical.)
